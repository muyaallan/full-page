Built for people who work with web content

Copy-Proof Tools began as an internal utility for the MAKE ENT STUDIO team. We work with research, references, and long articles daily — and we got tired of fighting pages that block selection, hide text behind overlays, or refuse to print cleanly.

What we ended up with is a browser toolkit that does three things extremely well:

1. Text on your terms

Whether you're citing a research paper, saving a product spec, or capturing a paragraph from a paywalled news site, the extension gives you the text — clean, plain, and copied through the extension's own clipboard pipeline so page scripts can't intercept it.

  • Select text → press Alt+H to highlight it
  • Right-click → Copy article text for main content only
  • Right-click → Copy full page text for the entire readable page

2. Screenshots that actually capture the page

Most screenshot tools capture what's on screen. Copy-Proof Tools drives the page — scrolling from top to bottom, waiting for lazy-loaded images, hiding sticky headers that would repeat, and stitching everything into one clean PNG.

3. Drag and drop across the browser

Select any text, hover the drag handle, and pull it anywhere. Drop into another tab, or paste into a desktop app — the extension writes to the system clipboard the moment the drag starts, so both targets work.
